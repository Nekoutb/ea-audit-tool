Purpose & guidance
Objective of this working paper

Assess whether an auditor's expert is needed, and evaluate the expert's competence, capabilities and objectivity (ISA 620). Document the nature of the work and how it will be used as audit evidence.

  1. Identify areas where an expert is used (valuations, actuarial, IT…).
  2. Evaluate the expert's competence and objectivity.
  3. Agree the scope of the expert's work in writing.
  4. Conclude on the adequacy of the expert's work as evidence.
